# Raqeeb 4.0 — Industrial Equipment Monitoring Experiment

**Type:** Independent prototype · **Status:** Early exploration with simulated device data · **Source repository:** Not linked publicly.

## The operational problem

Unexpected equipment interruptions can affect production schedules, maintenance workload, and operating costs. I wanted to explore how data from everyday machines could help identify unusual conditions earlier.

## Prototype scope

The experiment combines a dashboard with simulated readings for **temperature, vibration, power, and runtime**, using an MQTT-based telemetry flow. The prototype explores anomaly alerts, monitoring multiple measurements, and estimating potential operational losses from interruptions.

This is a learning experiment; it has **not** been validated on industrial equipment, deployed in a factory, or proven to reduce failures.

## What I learned

- Defining useful monitoring signals before picking a predictive model
- Representing threshold and anomaly alerts in operational dashboards
- Translating raw measurements into actions a maintenance team can understand
- Being explicit about the difference between simulated data and verified field measurements

## Programme participation

Raqeeb 4.0 received **initial qualification** for the 1KMile programme. This is not a claim of completion, winning, investment, or commercial adoption.

## Next evidence needed

A documented demo using synthetic data, measurable evaluation of false alerts, data quality checks, and a privacy/security review before any public technical release.
